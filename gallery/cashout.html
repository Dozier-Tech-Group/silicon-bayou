Step 1 — Value starts on-chain (not USD yet)

If Silicon Bayou ever paid a bounty, it would show up as crypto on Robinhood Chain (or as a non-cash credit). That is not dollars in a bank. This alpha does not send any of it.

Step 2 — Move it to an exchange you already use

You would withdraw or bridge that asset to an exchange that can sell it — typically Robinhood or Coinbase, if and when they support the asset. That hop is not live here. There is no button that moves real funds.

Step 3 — Sell to USD, then to your bank

On the exchange, you would sell the asset for USD and withdraw to a linked bank account — the same way you cash out stock or crypto today. KYC, taxes, and exchange rules still apply. Do not send real money to try this.

Pass for Tester A: you can explain those three hops in your own words in under 15 minutes. File confusion as a GitHub issue on Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou.
