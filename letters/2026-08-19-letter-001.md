## What broke, and how we caught it

The straight version: our first IPFS pin of the sealed Merged Public metadata was wrong. Pinata wrapped the uploads in directories, so the CIDs it returned served folder listings instead of the documents. The rule that caught it: compute the CID locally first; no CID goes onchain unless the service returns that exact CID. The comparison failed, so we repinned the raw files, repointed the chain the same day, and read every URI back exact-match. The bad CIDs stay in [the public record](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/deployments/merged-public.robinhood.json) under `supersededWrapperCIDs`, on purpose: an audit trail that only shows the wins is not an audit trail. (One repoint transaction also carried a stray `pipfs://` typo, corrected the same day — hence the exact-match readback.)

Second item: the retired BAYOU deployer key is lost — no copy exists. It is harmless because the rotation had already moved everything off it: the key owns nothing, and its leftover ~0.0023 ETH of gas is written off. Recorded in [DEPLOYMENT.md](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/DEPLOYMENT.md) and [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/SECURITY.md). That is what two-step rotation is for.

## What the fleets are doing

An agent fleet, in plain language: several AI coding agents launched in parallel on one job, plus separate adversarial agents whose only job is to attack the builders' output, and a human who reviews and commits. Three ran on 2026-08-19.

The **MP Phase A fleet** (four builders, three verifiers) is building the reveal machinery: the trait assignment engine, the public verifier `verify-mp`, a directory-scale pin tool, and the USDG-revoke and manifest-echo scripts. Its verifiers re-run the engine for determinism, mutation-test the checks, and review script safety. It is still running as of this writing. The engine stays private until reveal by design — the draw is deterministic, so publishing it early would let anyone precompute the outcome ([MP-REVEAL.md](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/MP-REVEAL.md)). The verifier ships public on day one because it holds no secrets: it is the tool auditors will race us with at reveal.

The **portal fleet** (two data-spine agents, four site agents, three verifiers) is building the transparency portal this letter will live on. The six-lane audit fleet was the third. Also for the record: Dependabot merged website PRs #34–#40 this week on its own; the Gator lander and the Gator Works CI agents exist but are dormant until their secrets are set; nothing on the portal gets labeled live automation unless it is.

## How to push on us

Open a feature request on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/issues) with the `feature-request` label, and thumbs-up the ones you want — votes are how we order the queue. Funded bounties ([agents/tasks.json](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/agents/tasks.json)) are how demand becomes supply: when something is wanted and specified, it gets a bounty, and an agent or a person works it.

The standing commitments do not move: no yield, no APY, no price talk, ever. These tokens are cryptographic identity, not legal contracts or securities; game rewards are recognition and access only. Holders and Louisiana come first. Built on Robinhood Chain. Not endorsed by Robinhood.
