## What broke, and how it was caught

Nothing broke this week, because nothing moved. The absence of breakage is not the same as health — it is the absence of activity, which is its own thing to report plainly rather than dress up.

## What the fleets did

No fleet launched this week. The standing automation is unchanged from [`portal/fleets.json`](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/portal/fleets.json): Dependabot is the only thing actually running, and it did exactly what it's scoped to do — watch the manifests, open a PR when a dependency moves. [`portal/status.json`](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/portal/status.json) has not been touched since 2026-08-19, which means every phase gate it records — Season Zero's treasury echo, art production, the rest of the Merged Public reveal — is exactly where letter 001 left it.

## The part of this letter that is itself the news

Three previous drafts of this letter are sitting unmerged. [Letter 002](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/pull/5) (opened 2026-08-24), [letter 003](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/pull/11) (opened 2026-09-21), and [letter 004](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/pull/12) (opened 2026-09-28) are all still open draft pull requests, each one written, each one waiting on the operator to fact-check and merge. Under [the rule this series runs on](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/letters/README.md), merging is publishing — a letter is not out until its PR lands, and its filename and date have to match the day it actually lands, not the day it was drafted. None of the four drafts now on file have merged. That is the real story of this quarter: not that the project broke, but that the publication pipeline for saying so, out loud, on a schedule, has itself backed up.

We are recording that plainly rather than letting it sit silently in a PR queue, because the whole point of this series is that the record does not get prettier than the repo.

## How to push on us

Open a feature request on [the issue tracker](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/issues) with the `feature-request` label, and thumbs-up the ones you want — votes are how we order the queue. Funded bounties ([agents/tasks.json](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/agents/tasks.json)) are how demand becomes supply.

The standing commitments do not move: no yield, no APY, no price talk, ever. These tokens are cryptographic identity, not legal contracts or securities. Holders and Louisiana come first. Built on Robinhood Chain. Not endorsed by Robinhood.
