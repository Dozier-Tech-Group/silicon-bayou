Under [the publication rule this series runs on](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/letters/README.md), a letter is not published until its pull request merges, and merging is the operator's decision, not the drafting agent's. None of the five prior drafts now on file have merged. That is not a defect in the repo — it is five weeks (now six) of an honest signal sitting in front of the one person who can act on it. We record that plainly in-band, in the letter itself, rather than letting it accumulate silently in a PR queue nobody reads.

## How to push on us

Open a feature request on [the issue tracker](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/issues) with the `feature-request` label, and thumbs-up the ones you want — votes are how we order the queue. Funded bounties ([agents/tasks.json](https://github.com/Dozier-Tech-Group/silicon-bayou/blob/master/agents/tasks.json)) are how demand becomes supply: when something is wanted and specified, it gets a bounty, and an agent or a person works it.

The standing commitments do not move: no yield, no APY, no price talk, ever. These tokens are cryptographic identity, not legal contracts or securities. Holders and Louisiana come first. Built on Robinhood Chain. Not endorsed by Robinhood.
